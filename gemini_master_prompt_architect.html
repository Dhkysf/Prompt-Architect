① TOP — RULES THAT ANCHOR REASONING (OR PUT IN SYSTEM INSTRUCTION)
Who Gemini is. Short, specific.
Do's & don'ts, tone, how to handle gaps.
Exact shape of the answer.
② MIDDLE — DATA, NOT INSTRUCTIONS
Long material goes here, before the question.
③ BOTTOM — THE ASK
One clear request.
Anchor + stop condition, at the very end.
Gemini-specific: one consistent format (XML tags or Markdown headings, never mixed) · rules & format at the top · long context in the middle · the question at the end, starting with "Based on the context above..."
